RapperHunt privacy policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Contact: madebybigb@gmail.com

RapperHunt is a Chrome extension for beatmakers. It finds similar Spotify artists in a listener range, saves prospects, and helps you copy DMs. This policy describes what the extension stores and what it sends over the network.

Summary

- We do not sell your personal data.

- Hunt and unlock features need a small amount of data on our Cloudflare Worker.

- Purchases and license keys are handled by Gumroad; card details never pass through the extension.

- The overlay runs on Spotify pages only.

Data stored on your device

RapperHunt uses Chrome storage and a local IndexedDB cache on your computer:

DataWhy
Settings (producer name, genre, DM templates, companion URL)Your setup
Prospects list (artist names, links, notes, follow-up dates)CRM on-device
Device idQuota and license seats
Install token (after unlock)Bind paid access to this Chrome install
Search presets, panel UI state, skip keysConvenience
Discovery cache (artists / recommendations)Faster hunts offline-ish

You can clear this by removing the extension or clearing site data for the extension in Chrome.

Data sent to RapperHunt servers

Default API: https://rapperhunt-api.bmbergman03.workers.dev (Cloudflare Worker + KV).

Typical requests include:

- Device id and install token (headers) for quota and paid seats

- License key once when you unlock (redeem)

- Hunt: seed artist / name, listener min/max, exclude ids

- Enrich: artist name / Spotify id for socials and previews

- Feedback: save / skip / not-similar signals (seed + artist ids/names)

We use this to enforce trial and paid hunt limits, seats (up to 2 installs), and to improve matching quality. We do not use it for advertising networks.

Opening Gumroad or social links uses your browser; we do not receive your Gumroad password or card number.

Host page (Spotify)

On Spotify artist pages, the overlay may read the page URL locally so Finder can seed that artist. That URL is used in the extension UI; it is not sold.

Cross-promotion

While a hunt loads, RapperHunt may show Sponsored links to other madebybigb products (AudioMark, VideoMark, MarkBundle) on Gumroad. Those are optional; they do not change hunt results.

Retention

Device and license seat records live in our Worker KV while needed for billing and abuse prevention. Local data stays until you clear it or uninstall.

Your choices

- Use the free trial without purchasing.

- Do not paste a license key if you do not want paid unlock.

- Uninstall the extension to stop collection from this browser.

- Email madebybigb@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests related to Worker-stored device/license records (include enough detail to find the record; we cannot recover keys from chat alone).
