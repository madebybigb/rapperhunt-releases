ProducerHunt privacy policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026
Contact: madebybigb@gmail.com

ProducerHunt is a Chrome extension that helps beatmakers find producer accounts in Instagram follower lists, save prospects, and copy outreach DMs. It is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Hunt results, notes, and settings stay on your device by default.
  • The overlay and hunt run on Instagram pages only, while you are logged in.
  • No hunt data is sent to ProducerHunt servers. License redeem sends the key to our Worker so we can bind up to 2 installs.

Data stored on your device

ProducerHunt uses Chrome local storage on your computer:

Uninstall the extension or clear extension data in Chrome to delete this.

What the extension reads on Instagram

After you agree in the extension UI and start a hunt, ProducerHunt uses your logged-in Instagram tab to load public profile fields and follower lists for the seed account you typed. It matches usernames and display names against your keywords. Login cookies stay in the Instagram tab; the extension does not copy session cookies to our servers.

Profile photos may be fetched from Instagram’s image CDN so they can display in the panel. Copy DM only writes text to your clipboard on this device.

Data sent over the network

Hunt results stay on this device. They are not uploaded.

Unlock uses our Cloudflare Worker (producerhunt-api.bmbergman03.workers.dev). When you paste a license key, the extension sends that key, a random device id, and an install token so we can verify the purchase with Gumroad and bind up to 2 Chrome installs. We do not receive your Instagram session or prospect list.

Optional /health and /config calls load default seed/keyword lists. That config does not include your prospects or hunt results.

Opening an Instagram profile, Gumroad checkout, a sponsored product link, or the privacy policy uses your browser like any other link. Card details stay on Gumroad.

Sponsored links

While a hunt runs, the panel may show a labeled Sponsored card for another madebybigb product (for example AudioMark). You can turn sponsored cards off in Settings. Those links do not change hunt results. We do not sell hunt data to advertisers.

Retention

Local data stays until you clear it or uninstall. We do not keep a copy of your prospects.

Your choices

  • Do not paste a license key if you do not want paid unlock.
  • Do not start a hunt if you do not want follower lists read in this browser.
  • Turn off sponsored cards in Settings.
  • Uninstall to stop the overlay on Instagram.
  • Email madebybigb@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
